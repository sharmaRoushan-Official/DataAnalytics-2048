8. Types of Data Analysis
---------------------------------------
There are mainly four common categories:

1. DESCRIPTIVE ANALYTICS

Core Question: What happened?
Purpose: Summarizes historical or current data to give a snapshot of performance.
Common Techniques: Data aggregation, mean/median calculations, and KPI dashboards.
Example: A monthly revenue report showing total sales or a website traffic summary.

2. DIAGNOSTIC ANALYTICS

Core Question: Why did it happen?
Purpose: Investigates anomalies, patterns, or root causes behind the results shown in descriptive data.
Common Techniques: Drill-down analysis, cohort segmentation, and correlation analysis.
Example: Looking into a sudden drop in sales to find out it was caused by a delayed product delivery or a regional marketing failure.

3. PREDICTIVE ANALYTICS

Core Question: What is likely to happen?
Purpose: Uses historical patterns and statistical models to forecast future trends or events.
Common Techniques: Regression analysis, machine learning, and time-series modeling.
Example: Forecasting next quarter's inventory demand or predicting which customers are at risk of churning.

4. PRESCRIPTIVE ANALYTICS

Core Question: What should we do next?
Purpose: Recommends specific actions or optimization strategies based on the prior three analytical stages.
Common Techniques: Optimization models, scenario simulations, and decision trees.
Example: Automatically adjusting marketing budgets or calculating the exact number of product units to stock for peak weeks.
